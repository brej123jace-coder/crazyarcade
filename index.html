The cabinets

Everything on the floor is coin-op original or a faithful restoration. No emulators pretending to be cabinets.

01

Galaxy Raiders

Vertical Shooter · 1982

The cabinet that started the collection. Twin joysticks, a screen full of enemy waves, and a high score table that's been running since we opened.

02

Neon Tank Corps

Combat / Maze · 1984

Drive a tank through a wire-frame maze and outlast the other tanks. Two-player mode turns friends into rivals fast.

03

Pixel Fury

Beat 'em Up · 1989

Four-player brawler with a co-op mode that gets loud. Our most fought-over cabinet on tournament nights.

04

Turbo Wedge GP

Racing · 1991

Sit-down cabinet with a force-feedback wheel that still kicks. Hold the record here and your name stays lit for a month.

05

Skyline Pinball

Pinball · 1986

A city-themed table with three ramps and a multiball mode most people never reach. Ask staff for the trick to the left flipper.

06

Void Runner

Vector Shooter · 1980

Glowing vector graphics on a genuine XY monitor. Simple rules, brutal difficulty curve, and a cult following at the lounge.
